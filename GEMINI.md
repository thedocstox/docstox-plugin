# DocStoX

The `docstox` MCP server gives you data on Indian stocks listed on NSE and BSE: prices, financials, ownership, screens, market flows, F&O and news. Every tool is read-only.

- The `read_me` tool returns the full catalogue of tools grouped by area.
- Symbols are NSE tickers such as `RELIANCE`, `TCS` or `HDFCBANK`. Use `search_stocks` to resolve a company name or ISIN.
- Amounts are in Indian rupees; market capitalisation and flows are in ₹ crore. Quote figures with their unit and as-of date.
- DocStoX covers Indian-listed companies only. Some research tools are DocStoX Pro; a locked result explains what Pro adds.
- The `skills/` folder in this extension describes good workflows for researching a stock, comparing stocks, screening, reviewing a portfolio and writing a market brief.
- DocStoX data is informational, not investment advice. Investment-related answers carry: "This is for informational purposes only and not investment advice. Please consult a SEBI-registered advisor before investing."
